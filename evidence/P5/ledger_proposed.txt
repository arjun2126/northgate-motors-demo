  - Batch_Number__c (Number, externalId) — sequence 1..20

  - Reset_At__c (DateTime) — when the batch was reset (null for active)
  - Status__c (Picklist) — Loaded | Partial | Reset | Archived
  - Notes__c (LongTextArea 1000) — free-form evidence
