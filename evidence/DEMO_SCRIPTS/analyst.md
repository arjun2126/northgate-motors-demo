# Analyst walkthrough — trustworthy pipeline and operations reporting

## 1. Activate persona

Sign in as the owner-approved existing VP/reporting identity in ng-demo and confirm actual
profile/role. Preserve the metrics-only and separate Finance/Service boundaries. Do not add a
PSG, switch to admin, assign a role or create a new analyst user when a report is denied.
An administrator may inspect report metadata separately, but that is not this persona's pass.

## 2. Establish the denominator and time

Open existing approved pipeline reports/dashboard surfaces only. Record report filters, running
user/context, grouping fields/lookup-target access and the report/cache timestamp. Do not assume
an old receipt's count is a current total. CP2 adjusted pricing and approvals are distinct from
standard list subtotal, invoicing, cash settlement or recognized revenue.

## 3. Interpret operations and exceptions honestly

Review only metrics actually granted to this identity. Published Executive_Service_Metrics__c
aggregates are deidentified snapshots and may be stale; scheduler activation/republish is not
authorized. Do not drill into private Service/Finance data to explain a tile. Zero CSI responses
mean no sample, not zero satisfaction; retain the documented 0-versus-N/A presentation exception.

Use historical source/report receipts to explain repeat purchase, managed nurture and loyalty
liability where the actual persona lacks direct access. Historical ledger totals are not current
stored balances; the program is inactive and legacy membership is not managed opt-in evidence.
Do not fabricate a positive sample, refresh data through a job or repair a discrepancy.

## 4. Phase 7 and product seams

Show the permission audit's assignment/group snapshot and its limits: unassigned definitions
are not proven unused permissions. The territory model has no assignments and remains Planning;
it does not justify new report access. Automotive/CPQ/loyalty/nurture notes label custom versus
licensed behavior. Agentforce, Data Cloud and portal screens are gated, not accepted demos.

## 5. Close and record

Record exact reports/filters/time/actual results and any denied or unavailable surface. Stop
that path on denial, retain the access exception and log out. No dashboard/folder/permission,
record, scheduler or approval changes. Persona/browser end-to-end acceptance is not signed off
until an owner reviews real observations; this prepared walkthrough is not that sign-off.
